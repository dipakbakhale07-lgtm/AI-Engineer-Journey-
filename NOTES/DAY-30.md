For each project explain:

1. Problem

## Reflection

### 10 Things I Learned

1. Building projects is more effective than only watching tutorials.
2. AI applications require more than an LLM API call.
3. Prompt engineering affects the quality of AI output.
4. RAG can provide relevant context to an LLM.
5. AI agents can combine reasoning with tools.
6. Structured outputs make AI workflows easier to process.
7. Testing and evaluation are necessary for reliable AI systems.
8. Git and GitHub are important parts of an engineering workflow.
9. Documentation makes technical work easier to understand.
10. Real projects reveal gaps in knowledge that tutorials may not show.

---

## 5 Mistakes I Made

1. Spending too much time trying to make everything perfect before testing.
2. Sometimes focusing on implementation before clearly defining the problem.
3. Not documenting some work immediately after completing it.
4. Spending too much time debugging without first isolating the problem.
5. Building features before validating whether they were actually necessary.
