# DAY 11 — Project 2: Design the Lead Agent

## Goal

Convert a traditional lead automation workflow into an AI-assisted workflow.

## What I Completed

- Created 20 dummy leads.
- Added name, course interest, message, city, and timeline fields.
- Defined SOC, Desktop, VAPT, and Other categories.
- Defined Hot, Warm, and Cold priority levels.
- Created clear classification rules.
- Defined a structured AI output schema.
- Designed the workflow for human review.

## Key Learning

An AI lead agent should not only assign labels.

It should provide a reason for its decision and recommend what should happen next.

Structured output makes AI decisions easier to review and use in automation workflows.

## Day 11 Deliverables

- Dummy lead dataset
- Classification rules
- AI output schema
- Practice and consistency review
