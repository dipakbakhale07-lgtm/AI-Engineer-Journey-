# Lead Classification Rules

## Category Labels

### SOC
Use **SOC** when the lead is interested in Security Operations Center, SOC analyst, security monitoring, or similar SOC-related training.

### Desktop
Use **Desktop** when the lead is interested in desktop support, IT support, helpdesk, hardware/software troubleshooting, or similar support work.

### VAPT
Use **VAPT** when the lead is interested in vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, ethical hacking, VAPT, or similar security testing.

### Other
Use **Other** when the lead does not clearly match SOC, Desktop, or VAPT.

---

## Priority Labels

### Hot
Use **Hot** when the lead shows strong buying or enrollment intent and is likely to take action soon.

Examples:
- Ready to enroll
- Wants to join
- Requests registration/admission details
- Requests a call for enrollment
- Wants to start immediately or very soon

### Warm
Use **Warm** when the lead clearly shows interest but still needs information or has a longer timeline.

Examples:
- Asking for fees
- Asking for syllabus or duration
- Asking about batches
- Asking about course details
- Interested but not ready to enroll yet

### Cold
Use **Cold** when the lead shows weak, unclear, exploratory, or distant interest.

Examples:
- Just exploring
- No clear timeline
- Not decided
- Plans to start much later
- Asks general questions without a clear intention to enroll

---

## Classification Rules

1. Determine the **category** primarily from the course interest and message.
2. Determine the **priority** from the strength of interest, enrollment intent, and timeline.
3. Explicit enrollment intent should carry more weight than a timeline alone.
4. Use **Other** when the category cannot be determined clearly.
5. Do not guess when the lead information is insufficient.
6. The classification should be understandable enough that two people applying these rules would usually reach the same result.

---

## Human Review

The AI classification is a recommendation for a human-reviewed workflow.

A human should be able to review:

- Category
- Priority
- Reason
- Recommended next action
- Draft reply

Important actions should not be taken automatically without human review.
