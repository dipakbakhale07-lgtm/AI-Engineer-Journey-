# Content Strategy — AI Social Media Assistant

## Project Goal

Develop a system that aids in crafting valuable social media posts, avoiding spam-like content.

The assistant's role is to facilitate the creation of informative, experience-driven content, while the human retains the ultimate authority to publish.

## Target Audience

The intended audience includes:

- Builders with an interest in AI
- Clients seeking AI projects
- Individuals curious about freelancing
- Those interested in Python projects

## Content Principles

Each planned post must have a clear justification for its existence.

Content should:

- Offer valuable information or a real learning opportunity.
- Stem from actual learning, building, or experiences.
- Be accessible to the intended readers.
- Refrain from generic motivational material.
- Exclude fabricated outcomes, successes, or experiences.
- Undergo review prior to publication.

## Four Post Types

### 1. Lessons I Gained

Objective:

Share a concept in AI, Python, or AI engineering that was genuinely acquired.

Instances:

- Insights on RAG
- Insights on embeddings
- Insights on prompt engineering
- Insights from troubleshooting a Python project

The article should clarify the lesson effectively and offer valuable insights to the audience.

### 2. What I Developed

Objective:

Demonstrate something that was truly constructed and describe its function.

Instances:

- AI Lead Qualification Agent
- Lead Management Dashboard
- RAG Knowledge Assistant
- Python AI project capabilities

The article should explain:

- What was constructed
- The issue it tackles
- Its general mechanism
- Insights gained during the process

### 3. What Went Wrong

Goal:

Share a real issue, error, or failed method and describe the takeaway.

Illustrations:

- A Python bug
- A flawed AI result
- A defect in a project
- A function that did not perform as intended

The emphasis should be on:

Issue → What occurred → Remedy or insight

The aim is to learn, not to conceal errors.

### 4. Simple Explanation

Goal:

Clarify a technical idea in straightforward terms so that a beginner can grasp it.

Examples:

- Can you define an API?
- Can you define an embedding?
- Can you define RAG?
- Can you define an AI agent?
- Can you define prompt engineering?

The explanation ought to be straightforward, practical, and scientifically sound.

## Human Approval Rule

No content will be published automatically.

Here is the process:

Content Idea
↓
AI creates an initial draft
↓
A person reviews the draft
↓
The person makes edits if needed
↓
The person gives final approval
↓
Manual publishing

AI only prepares the draft.

The human makes the final call on what gets published.

## Source Checking Rule

Before publishing, verify that the content is grounded in:

- Actual learning
- Actual projects
- Actual experiences
- Verified technical information

The system must not fabricate:

- Projects that were not built
- Results that were not achieved
- Experiences that did not happen
- Technical claims without support

## Quality Rule

A post should answer:

"Why is this useful to the reader?"

If a post lacks a useful lesson, project insight, explanation, or genuine experience, it should not be published.

## Content Quality Checklist

Before approval, review:

- Does the subject matter relate to the intended readers?
- Does the post offer valuable insights?
- Is it grounded in actual facts?
- Are the details correct?
- Is the wording easy to understand?
- Does the post avoid unsubstantiated statements?
- Was it reviewed by a person?

## Educational Purpose

This project is being developed for learning purposes as part of the AI Builder Challenge.

The aim is to grasp:

- Content planning
- AI-supported writing
- Human review
- Verifying sources
- Structuring content
- Effective social media engagement
